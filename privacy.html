Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

MerxyLab ("we", "us", "our") operates a Facebook Messenger chatbot to assist customers with product inquiries and orders. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

When you send a message to our Facebook Page, we may receive and temporarily process:

  • Your Facebook display name — used to address you in admin notifications.
  • Your Facebook Page-Scoped ID (PSID) — a unique identifier assigned by Facebook to identify your conversation. It cannot be used to identify you on other platforms.
  • Message content — the text you send to our Page, used to generate a reply.
  • Delivery address — only if you choose to provide it during an order flow.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, payment card details, or any other personal information unless you voluntarily provide them in a message.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your product and order inquiries via Messenger.
  • To route your conversation to a human admin when needed.
  • To send admin notifications (via Telegram) summarizing conversations for order fulfilment.
  • To temporarily remember your delivery address within a single session so you do not have to re-enter it.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to customer service.

3. Data Retention

  • In-memory session data (conversation history, address, message count) is cleared daily or when your conversation ends — whichever comes first.
  • Cooldown state (whether a human admin is handling your case) is stored in an encrypted Redis database with a 25-hour expiry.
  • We do not maintain a permanent database of customer conversations.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate the chatbot. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Meta (Facebook) — Messenger platform that delivers your messages to us. Meta Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — AI service that generates replies to your questions. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Upstash — Redis database used for short-term session state. Upstash Privacy Policy
  • Telegram — Used for internal admin alerts only. Customers do not interact with Telegram directly.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party except as described in Section 4 (service providers required to operate the chatbot) or when required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data we hold about you by contacting us. Because we do not maintain a permanent database, most data is automatically deleted within 25 hours.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our Messenger service after changes constitutes acceptance.
